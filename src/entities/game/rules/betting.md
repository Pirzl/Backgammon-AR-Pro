# Backgammon Betting Rules (Doubling Cube)

## The Doubling Cube

In backgammon, the "betting die" is actually called the **doubling cube**. It is not a traditional die with numbers 1 through 6, but rather it has the numbers **2, 4, 8, 16, 32, and 64**, which represent the multiplication of the initial bet.

### Face Values

These numbers indicate the value of the match or the multiplication of the base bet:

- **2**: Match is worth double.
- **4**: Match is worth four times.
- ...and so on up to 64.

---

## Doubling Mechanics

### Start of Gameplay

- The doubling cube starts **"neutral"** (belongs to neither player).
- It is placed in the center of the bar showing the number **64**.
- The value of the game is **1** (base bet).

### Making an Offer

- **Who**: Any player can offer to double **before rolling their dice** on their turn.
- **Frequency**: Only one offer can be made per turn.
- **Action**: The offering player places the cube on the opponent's side with the **next value** face up (e.g., flipping 64 to 2).
- **Meaning**: "I offer to play this game at double its current value."

### Responding to an Offer

The opponent has two options:

1.  **Take (Accept)**:
    - The game continues at the new, doubled value.
    - The cube moves to the **accepting player's side**.
    - **Ownership**: The accepting player now owns the exclusive right to offer the _next_ double.
2.  **Drop (Reject)**:
    - The opponent forfeits the game immediately.
    - They lose the **current value** of the game (before the proposed double).

### The Right to Double Later

- Once a player accepts a double, they **own the cube**.
- Only the owner can make the next offer to double (redouble).
- The process repeats (e.g., owner flips 2 to 4 and offers it back).

---

## Advanced Rules (Optional)

### Automatic Double-Up (Beavers & Raccoons)

Used in friendly games or specific variants for fast betting:

- **Beaver**: If a player is offered a double, they can immediately **redouble (Beaver)** while retaining ownership of the cube.
  - _Effect_: Accepts the double and immediately doubles it again.
- **Raccoon**: Some rules allow an immediate counter-redouble to a Beaver.

---

## Scoring Multipliers

The final score is determined by the **Cube Value** multiplied by the **Win Condition**.

### Win Conditions

1.  **Normal Win (x1)**: Winner bears off all checkers. Loser has borne off at least one.
2.  **Gammon (x2)**: Loser has **not removed any checkers**.
    - _Calculation_: Bet x 2.
3.  **Backgammon (x3)**: Loser has **not removed any checkers** AND still has checkers on the **winner's home board** or the **bar**.
    - _Calculation_: Bet x 3.

### Example Calculation

- Cube Value: **4**
- Win Condition: **Backgammon (x3)**
- **Total Score**: 4 x 3 = **12 points** (12x base bet).

---

## Practical Example

1.  **Start**: Game value is 1. Cube is neutral.
2.  **Turn 10 (Ana)**: Ana doubles to 2. Luis **Takes**.
    - Game Value: 2.
    - Cube Owner: Luis.
3.  **Turn 25 (Luis)**: Luis doubles to 4. Ana **Takes**.
    - Game Value: 4.
    - Cube Owner: Ana.
4.  **End Game**: Ana wins a **Normal Win**.
    - **Result**: Ana wins **4 points**.
